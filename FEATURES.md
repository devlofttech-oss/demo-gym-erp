# Kilos — Gym ERP Platform
### Everything your gym needs. One platform.

---

## Core Modules

### 1. Member Management
- Unlimited member profiles with photos, emergency contacts & health notes
- Status tracking — Active, Expiring, Expired, Frozen
- Bulk import via Excel
- Membership freeze & unfreeze with auto-resume date
- Member QR code for quick check-in
- Printable membership receipts
- Absentee detection (members absent 5+ days)
- SMS reminders for expiring memberships

### 2. Plans & Pricing
- Flexible plan types — Gym, Personal Training, Group Class, Day Pass, Add-on
- Set price, joining fee, duration, and custom feature list per plan
- Activate / deactivate plans anytime
- Auto-calculates correct expiry (month-accurate, not ×30 days)

### 3. Payments & Billing
- Record payments with mode — Cash, Card, UPI, Bank Transfer, Cheque
- Tracks total fees, amount paid, and outstanding balance
- Full payment history per member (editable & deletable)
- Discount support per transaction
- Printable receipt with gym branding & logo

### 4. Check-in & Attendance
- Phone or QR code check-in
- Dedicated kiosk / scanner mode for reception desk
- Visual attendance calendar per member
- Today's check-in list on dashboard
- Attendance history with delete support

### 5. Classes & Scheduling
- Group class management — Zumba, Yoga, Dance, HIIT, Kids Dance, Gym & more
- Trainer assignment per class
- Schedule with days & times
- Enrolled members vs capacity tracking (progress bar)

### 6. Staff Management
- Roles — Trainer, Staff, Manager, Receptionist
- Salary & commission (flat or percentage)
- Certifications tracking
- Optional login account per staff member
- Photo upload support

### 7. Personal Training (PT)
- PT package management with session counts
- Session scheduling (Scheduled / Completed / Cancelled)
- Per-member session tracking with trainer notes
- Active package dashboard with completion stats

### 8. Renewals
- Smart renewal queue grouped by urgency (Overdue → Today → 1–3 days → 4–7 days → 8–30 days)
- One-click renewal from the queue
- Frozen membership management with resume date

### 9. Leads & CRM
- Lead pipeline — New → Contacted → Follow-up → Interested → Won / Lost
- Source tracking — Walk-in, Phone, WhatsApp, Website, Referral
- Assigned-to staff, potential value, follow-up date
- Conversion rate metrics
- Today's leads & monthly leads at a glance

### 10. Communication Hub
- Broadcast announcements — SMS, Email, WhatsApp, In-App
- Target by audience — All Members, Active Only, Expired Members
- Announcement types — General, Promotion, Alert, Event
- Follow-up task manager — Renewal Reminder, Payment Due, Birthday, Custom
- Assign follow-ups to staff with due dates & completion tracking

### 11. Supplements Inventory
- Category-wise inventory — Protein, Creatine, Pre-Workout, Vitamins, Fat Burner, BCAA
- Stock status badges — In Stock, Low Stock, Out of Stock
- Expiry date tracking with alerts (expired / expiring within 30 days)
- Sell & Restock modals with stock auto-update
- Low stock alerts (< 10 units)

### 12. Expense Tracking
- Categorised expenses — Rent, Electricity, Salaries, Equipment, Supplements, Marketing & more
- Recurring expense flag
- Per-category monthly budget targets
- Actual vs budget bar chart
- CSV export
- Monthly & yearly filtering

### 13. Equipment Maintenance
- Equipment register with purchase price & date
- Service date tracking with visual alerts — Overdue (red), Due soon (amber), OK (green)
- Maintenance scheduling with notes

### 14. Measurements & Progress
- Weight & body measurement log per member
- Date-wise history sorted newest first
- Notes per entry

### 15. Workout Plans
- Library of workout plans — Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
- Exercise list with sets, reps & duration
- Fitness goal tagging

### 16. Diet & Nutrition
- Diet plan builder — Weight Loss, Muscle Gain, Maintenance, Medical
- Macro targets — Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat
- Assign to a specific member or save as reusable template
- Meal timing & restriction notes

### 17. Reports
- Monthly revenue vs expenses bar chart
- New members, renewals & leads per month trend
- Top performing plans by revenue
- Lead conversion & source breakdown
- PDF & Excel export

### 18. Dashboard
- Live stats — Revenue (today / this month), Active members, Check-ins
- Expiring members alert panel (next 7 days)
- Revenue trend area chart
- Revenue by plan breakdown
- 6-month revenue vs expenses comparison
- Recent activity feed
- Quick actions — New Member, Record Payment, Check-in, Renewals

### 19. Settings
- Gym name, location & contact details
- Light / Dark theme
- All settings per gym (multi-tenant isolated)

## Platform Highlights

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Multi-gym | Each gym's data is fully isolated — one platform, many branches |
| Role-based access | Admin, Staff, and Super Admin roles with separate views |
| Dark mode | Full dark theme across all modules |
| Mobile-ready | Responsive design — works on tablet & phone |
| QR Check-in | Member QR code + dedicated kiosk scanner screen |
| Bulk import | Excel upload for onboarding existing members |
| SMS integration | Renewal reminders & payment alerts via SMS |
| Cloud-backed | Firebase Firestore — real-time, no server maintenance |
| Receipt printing | Branded PDF receipt with gym logo |
| CSV & PDF export | Expenses, reports, receipts |
